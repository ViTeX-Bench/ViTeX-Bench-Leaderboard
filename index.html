    How it is scored

    Thirteen metrics on three axes. One primary metric per axis; the chart uses those three.

    Pareto front. A raw editor is on the front unless another raw editor is at least as good on all three primary metrics and better on one. Setting the front aside and repeating gives front 2, front 3, and so on. It is computed in three dimensions, so a front member can look dominated in a two-axis view.

    Not ranked. Post-processed outputs and the Source video are shown for reference only. Methods marked † are left out of temporal comparisons.

    Submit a result

    Anyone can submit. A maintainer reviews each result before it appears here.

    1. 1

      Run the evaluation code on the frozen split of ViTeX-Dataset. It writes eval.json.

    2. 2

      Open a submission issue with eval.json attached. Note any post-processing or frame interpolation.

    3. 3

      After review, your method appears in the chart and the table.
